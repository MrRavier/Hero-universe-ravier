# Mihail returns to Earth and the Host dissolves — Sahara, 2022

Immediately after the destruction of the last Superior in the adjacent fourth-dimensional world, Mihail returns by teleport to Earth, choosing the exact location in the Sahara where he had disappeared.

His return is abrupt and physically difficult. He reappears exhausted, burned, bloodied, with damaged field gear and residual shielding from the fourth-dimensional detonation. He has survived, but only barely.

At effectively the same moment, a global collapse begins across the Host.

The effect is not limited to the Sahara. It happens across all active fronts and all surviving manifested Host forms on Earth.

Observed sequence:

- Host formations lose coordination almost instantly.
- Individuals stagger, freeze, lash out, convulse, or enter disorganized erratic behavior.
- Larger forms and command organisms fail in progressively visible ways.
- Aerial forms lose controlled flight; some spiral or crash before complete dissolution.
- Living organic weapons and human-derived forms begin to lose structural cohesion.
- The dissolution spreads rapidly through all Host categories.
- Bodies become ash-like particulate.
- The ash itself then continues to disintegrate until nothing material remains.

The disappearance is therefore total: no stable corpse, biological tissue, ash, mineral plate, weapon-organism, or usable Host residue remains.

This includes the forces in the Sahara and the forces deployed to the other newly opened fronts.

The collapse strongly indicates that the last Superior was not merely a political commander but a metaphysical or systemic continuity point for the Host. Exactly why his death causes total erasure remains unknown at this moment.

Important canon result:

- Mihail returns alive to Earth.
- The last Superior is dead.
- The Host begins failing everywhere immediately after that death.
- All Host forms ultimately dissolve into ash and then disappear completely, leaving no physical Host remains.
- The exact deeper mechanism for the Host's total disappearance remains unrevealed unless later established.
