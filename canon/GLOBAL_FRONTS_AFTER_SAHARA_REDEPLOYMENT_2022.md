# Global fronts after the Sahara redeployment — 2022

After the Superior rose in the Sahara and the Living Structures vanished from the immediate battlefield, the war ceased to be a single decisive concentration. Several Living Structures reappeared across the world, opening or reopening fronts designed to divide human attention and force national militaries and extraordinary responders to fight on multiple continents at once.

The Sahara remained the symbolic and strategic center because the Last Superior himself was present there, together with the pseudo-Kheruun court, the 30-meter modified Kheruun corpse/Behemoth, Aberrant command forms and a still-massive Host army. But Earth could no longer commit everything to one desert.

## East Asia — Korean Peninsula

A Living Structure manifested in the Yellow Sea approaches west of the Korean Peninsula, close enough to threaten both Korean states and regional shipping. It opened by releasing aerial hunting forms, war carriers and amphibious-derived creatures toward coastal and inland routes.

South Korean aircraft and air-defense units engaged immediately. North Korean formations, already politically committed to the anti-Host fight after Sahara, opened their own fires from the north. Japanese aircraft and ships began engaging aerial forms before they could disperse farther east.

The front became notable because enemies who would normally watch one another through sights were instead firing in the same direction at the same alien mass.

## South America — Brazil

A Living Structure appeared over sparsely populated inland terrain in northeastern Brazil, later partially grounding itself and releasing large numbers of ground forms. Brazilian Army, Air Force, extraordinary military assets and civilian heroes responded before the Host could reach major population centers.

Jaguará was still in Sahara at this moment, so Brazil's domestic front was handled by forces and heroes who had not been sent away. The visual contrast became iconic: armored columns and low-flying attack aircraft over dry interior terrain against pale organic siege forms moving through dust and brush.

## North America — western Canada

A Living Structure manifested in remote western Canada, forcing Pacific Response and Canadian authorities to retain domestic extraordinary personnel who had not traveled to Sahara. This was one of the concrete reasons Thomas Mercer had not been present in the initial Sahara concentration.

The Canadian front emphasized powered responders fighting alongside conventional forces rather than celebrity-style heroics. Several old Northbound-associated responders remained in-country because Canada could not abandon its own territory while the global battle continued.

## Indian subcontinent

Another Structure appeared over western India, producing a fast-moving mixed Host column of ground and aerial forms. Indian military aviation, artillery and extraordinary state personnel responded rapidly. The front was dense, violent and less static than the first Texas/Xinjiang/France pattern: the Host attempted to create multiple moving axes rather than one fixed siege line.

## Eastern Mediterranean / Anatolian approaches

A smaller but aggressive Living Structure appeared in the eastern Mediterranean region and began releasing aerial and transport forms toward coastal states. Turkish, Greek and allied aircraft were forced into the same battlespace. Naval air defense and ship-launched missiles became central almost immediately.

This front directly complicated reinforcement routes toward Sahara, because aircraft and ships moving toward North Africa now had to account for an active Host presence in the broader Mediterranean theater.

## East Africa

A Living Structure appeared inland in East Africa, producing a lower-density but highly mobile Host force. The threat was not initially raw mass but speed: hunting forms, transport organisms and light war forms spreading across large distances. Regional militaries and African heroes began fighting a more mobile campaign than the dense Sahara confrontation.

## Character of the global war

The result was exactly what the Superior had claimed: humanity was many and geographically divided. But his conclusion was only half true. The same division that made human command messy also meant Earth possessed thousands of independent centers of resistance. Destroying or isolating one country did not end the war.

National chains of command remained intact. There was no single Earth army. Yet combat data from Sahara, Texas, France and Xinjiang began moving quickly between fronts: known weak points, behavior of Pretorians, air-form patterns, Living Structure internals, and Mihail's limited translated psionic packet.

The war therefore split into two simultaneous realities:

- Sahara: the Last Superior, Behemoth, pseudo-Kheruun court, major heroes and the most symbolically important battlefield on Earth.
- Global fronts: multiple Living Structures forcing nations to defend their own territory while still feeding personnel, aircraft, missiles and intelligence toward Sahara whenever possible.

## Sahara meanwhile

The battle did not slow. Leone continued fighting the 30-meter Behemoth after destroying the winged Host commander. Thomas Mercer and Mihail fought side by side through the ground Host. The Superior entered the field personally but refused banal direct combat, instead using masses of pseudo-Kheruun clinging to his limbs as delegated striking extensions.

The human side had received major reinforcement, but the sudden world-wide redeployment meant that additional forces intended for Sahara were now being diverted, delayed or recalled. The desert battle was no longer the only place where Earth could lose.
