# Alien baseline — Kheruun are not the only extraterrestrials

**Status: active shared-universe canon.**

This document fixes a continuity point that must not be lost when developing the Kheruun/Host material.

## Multiple extraterrestrial species exist

The Kheruun are **not** the only alien or non-human extraterrestrial species in the universe.

Multiple extraterrestrial species, civilizations, groups and individuals exist. Most have not yet been developed in detail.

The Kheruun are simply the first non-human civilization to receive extensive narrative development in this continuity.

## Earth is not the center of the universe

Earth is not cosmically unique or the secret center of all non-human activity.

Different alien groups may observe, visit, infiltrate, trade, interfere, hide, flee, settle temporarily or pursue unrelated agendas.

Their existence does not imply one shared empire, one shared origin or one universal alien conspiracy.

## Contact before 2019

Alien contact with Earth did **not** begin with the Host War.

There have been earlier contacts, presences or interventions that were limited, hidden, misunderstood, classified, localized or otherwise not globally accepted as proof of extraterrestrial civilization.

Therefore:

- 2019 is not the first instant any alien ever touched Earth;
- it is the first **massive, globally undeniable alien crisis/contact** of the current era;
- the Kheruun invasion makes the existence of non-human intelligent civilization impossible for the world to dismiss afterward.

## Kheruun-specific facts

The Kheruun remain distinct from other aliens.

Their established traits include:

- origin in a fourth-dimensional world adjacent to Earth;
- a civilization devastated by its own terminal internal annihilation;
- metaphysical/cultural rejection of banality;
- ancient removal and remodeling of primitive human lineages into functional biological forms;
- the Host as a system of derived tools, organisms, structures and war-forms;
- the last Superior as the final known true Kheruun associated with the invading Host.

These facts must not be generalized automatically to other alien species.

## Habilidades are not explained by aliens

The existence of extraterrestrials does not create a universal alien origin for human Habilidades.

Habilidades predate the public 2019 crisis and remain a broad feature of the setting with no single mandatory explanation.

## Possible alien roles

Future alien characters may be, among other things:

- heroes;
- villains;
- refugees;
- soldiers;
- merchants;
- explorers;
- scientists;
- criminals;
- diplomats;
- ordinary civilians.

Being alien is not itself a moral category.

## Continuity rule

Future stories must not describe the Kheruun as “the aliens” in a way that implies they are the only extraterrestrial intelligence in existence.

The Host War is a uniquely important public event because of its scale and visibility, not because it proves that nothing alien existed before it.
