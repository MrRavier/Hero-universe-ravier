# Mihail recovery, classified debrief and postwar transition — 2022

## Continuity basis
This file follows the Host War victory, Mihail's return from the adjacent fourth-dimensional Kheruun world, the dissolution of the Host, and the initial debrief canon.

Important continuity:
- Extraordinary science, medicine, law, hero professions and state use of powered people already existed long before 2022.
- The Host War expands and redirects mature extraordinary institutions; it does not create them from zero.
- Mihail's first battlefield statement after returning was simply that he killed the Superior.
- The broad truth of the fourth-dimensional duel and catastrophic energy release is disclosed later in closed debriefs.
- The exact atomic mechanism is not made public.
- Aleksandar is a toddler in 2022 and is deliberately protected from propaganda.
- Sintica's Monarquia Absoluta de Emergência remains temporarily in force during immediate recovery, but the end of the existential threat makes the constitutional question unavoidable.

## First days after victory
Mihail spends the first days after the Sahara battle under intensive medical observation. He has severe exhaustion, blunt-force trauma, burns, soft-tissue damage, magical/extraordinary overuse, and unknown exposure from the Kheruun environment and his own final energy event. His dense skeleton helps explain survival of some impacts but does not protect soft tissue.

He is conscious intermittently at first. Dora, Katalin and close family are given access in controlled periods. Aleksandar's visit remains intimate and brief.

The world outside is already celebrating victory, but Mihail is physically incapable of participating in ceremonies.

## Classified debrief
Once coherent enough, Mihail gives a formal account to a small Sintican and allied circle. He does not provide a reproducible engineering recipe.

He confirms:
- he deliberately used the Superior's exposed body as a teleport anchor/reference into the adjacent fourth-dimensional Kheruun world;
- the transfer placed Mihail near the ground and the Superior in motion above it, causing the Superior to fall violently on arrival;
- he fought the Superior directly for an extended period;
- while using Psique on himself, he had recognized that his previously named "Solid Oxygen" ability was misclassified and represented a broader capacity to manipulate matter at chemical and molecular scales;
- under extreme strain he reached an atomic-scale edge of that ability;
- he used that edge in a fictional, non-engineering, non-reproducible manner to trigger a catastrophic nuclear-like energy release around the Superior;
- post-event comparison places the scale around 100 megatons TNT-equivalent, though the fourth-dimensional environment prevents exact Earth-physics reconstruction;
- he escaped more than one hundred kilometres away using teleportation, improvised dense shielding, Éter and distance;
- after the blast he returned close enough to verify the outcome;
- the blackened remnant of the Superior rose, tried to speak, failed, then lost cohesion and became ash;
- Mihail then returned to Sahara, after which Host forms worldwide destabilized, became incoherent, turned to dust and vanished entirely.

Mihail repeatedly emphasizes that he does not know whether he can reproduce the atomic-scale event safely or predictably. He does not claim stable mastery.

## Scientific interpretation
Existing extraordinary-science institutions treat the event as a new extreme case within a mature field, not as the birth of a new science. Laboratories and universities already studied abilities, extraordinary biology, unusual materials, magical traditions, powered medicine and other anomalous phenomena before 2022.

What changes is priority. Kheruun dimensional research becomes a new high-priority subfield joining existing extraordinary studies, physics, topology, cognition, magical research and teleportation theory.

Mihail's case becomes uniquely sensitive because his abilities cross several domains at once: teleportation, Psique, Éter, magic and matter manipulation.

## Heroic ecosystem after the war
The war does not create heroes, licensing, academies, reserves or extraordinary-response doctrine. Those institutions already had long histories.

Instead, governments conduct postwar reviews of interoperability, existential-threat planning, cross-border liaison, powered reserve mobilization, civil-military coordination and legal limits on coercive recruitment. Different countries respond differently.

There is no single global hero authority created from the Host War.

## Public information
The public is told that Mihail isolated and killed the last Superior in its own world and that a catastrophic final energy event occurred there. The existence of the event is not denied, because battlefield timing, Mihail's injuries and his own later statements make a total cover story implausible.

However, the exact atomic aspect and method are initially withheld. Public language uses phrases such as "catastrophic energy release" or "final extraordinary discharge" rather than a technical description.

Rumours that Mihail produced something equivalent to a strategic nuclear explosion spread quickly, but no government initially confirms the mechanism.

## Family
Mihail's private recovery is deliberately separated from public mythmaking. Aleksandar is not displayed as a symbolic child of victory. Dora and the family refuse staged father-son imagery during the first recovery period.

For Mihail, Aleksandar becomes an increasingly concrete reason not to let postwar Sintica become a permanent emergency state or personal cult.

## Sintica and the constitutional question
The Monarquia Absoluta de Emergência continues temporarily for immediate continuity: repatriation, casualty accounting, medical treatment, security, damaged state functions and international negotiations.

However, with the Superior dead and the Host gone, the original existential justification no longer supports indefinite continuation. Ministers, jurists, royal advisers, political factions and civil society begin preparing for the question of what comes next.

No automatic restoration or permanent absolutism is established at this point. The decision remains pending Mihail's recovery and explicit political action.

## Diplomatic consequence
Foreign governments now regard Mihail as both the individual most directly responsible for ending the Host War and a strategic unknown. His importance increases Sintica's diplomatic weight but does not transform the principality into a conventional military or economic superpower.

The distinction matters: Sintica remains a small European state; Mihail is an exceptionally powerful sovereign within it.
