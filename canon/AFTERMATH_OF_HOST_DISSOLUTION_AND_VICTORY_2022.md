# Aftermath of the Host Dissolution and Victory (2022)

Immediately after Mihail Orbelos returns to the Sahara and confirms that he killed the last Superior, the entire Host collapses across Earth. Host forms first lose coordination, then become erratic, then disintegrate into dark dust. The dust itself subsequently vanishes until no Host material remains. This occurs globally and affects ordinary war forms, Praetorians, Aberrant Generals, aerial forms, the Behemoth, and other living imperial constructs.

The end is not instantaneous psychologically. Human forces initially keep weapons raised because no one trusts the silence. Command centers around the world require repeated sector-by-sector confirmation before anyone accepts that there are no remaining Host contacts.

In the Sahara, Thomas Mercer is among the first to reach Mihail after the collapse begins. Mihail is barely upright after the fourth-dimensional battle. Thomas asks what happened, and Mihail answers simply that he killed the Superior. Leone lands shortly afterward, heavily exhausted from the fight against the Behemoth, and understands what Mihail did without needing a detailed explanation.

The first confirmed global declaration is not a triumphal political speech but an operational statement: no Hostiles remain confirmed on Earth. Only after this do human soldiers, heroes, pilots, medics, and civilians begin to celebrate, cry, pray, collapse from exhaustion, or embrace one another.

Mihail himself does not celebrate. He collapses from accumulated trauma, burns, blood loss, exhaustion, magical and psionic overuse, teleport strain, and exposure to the fourth-dimensional battle. He is immediately treated as a critical casualty rather than as a ceremonial victor.

The victory is understood as collective. Mihail killed the last Superior, but he could only reach that point because thousands of soldiers, heroes, responders, pilots, medics, and states held the line long enough for him to do it. Leone's battle against the winged commander and Behemoth, Thomas's assistance opening the Superior's servant armor, and the global military resistance remain central parts of the victory narrative.

The long-term meaning of the event remains open, but the immediate fact is absolute: the Host is gone, the last Superior is dead, and Earth survives.
