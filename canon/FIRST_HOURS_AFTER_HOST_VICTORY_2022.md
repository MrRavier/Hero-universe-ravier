# First Hours After the Host Victory (2022)

Canonical continuation of the immediate aftermath after Mihail Orbelos returns from the fourth-dimensional Kheruun world and the entire Host dissolves from Earth.

## No instant celebration everywhere

The Host's disappearance is nearly simultaneous across the planet, but confirmation is not. Military commands maintain weapons hot, air patrols continue, medical triage expands, and extraordinary responders keep searching former front zones. The absence of hostile contacts is verified front by front rather than assumed from Sahara alone.

The first global operational truth is simple: the invasion force is gone, but the damage remains.

## Sahara

Mihail loses consciousness shortly after confirming to Thomas Mercer that he killed the last Superior. He is evacuated under heavy medical guard. Leone and Thomas remain nearby during the first transfer, both wounded and exhausted.

The Sahara battlefield becomes a surreal mix of victory and catastrophe: surviving soldiers embrace, some laugh, some pray, some simply sit in the sand, while medics continue treating casualties and recovery teams search wreckage where the Host had existed minutes earlier.

No Host bodies remain. No ash remains. Only human wreckage, damaged ground, abandoned ammunition, destroyed aircraft and vehicles, and the marks of the battle remain.

## Global reaction

As front commands confirm zero Host contacts, governments begin cautious victory statements. Public celebrations erupt spontaneously but are interwoven with mourning. Churches, mosques, temples and public squares fill. Sirens sound in some cities; bells ring in others.

The phrase from Mihail's final transmission — "Paz para Sintica. Paz para o mundo." — spreads globally, first as a plea associated with what many believed to be his last words, then as a victory phrase once his survival is confirmed.

## Sintica

Kerasia enters a state between celebration and vigil. The Palace confirms only that Mihail has returned alive, is severely injured, and is receiving care. Dora, Katalin, Maria, Nikola and Sofia are informed before a fuller public bulletin.

No triumphalist palace ceremony is held in the first hours. The principality's authorities emphasize the dead and wounded first.

## Political reality

Mihail's survival immediately creates a constitutional question that cannot be ignored. He remains the sovereign who had concentrated emergency absolute power during the Host war. Victory does not automatically settle whether those powers continue.

That debate does not begin publicly in the first hour because he is unconscious, but ministers, jurists and members of the court already understand that the end of the existential emergency removes the clearest justification for indefinite continuation of wartime absolutism.

## Heroic world

The victory permanently changes Mihail's status in the global extraordinary community. He is no longer merely a sovereign with powerful abilities or an unusual field actor. He is the person who isolated and killed the last Superior, after which the Host collapsed everywhere.

Leone remains one of the most visible battlefield heroes of the war, particularly for defeating the winged commander and holding the Behemoth. Thomas Mercer's second-wave arrival and side-by-side fight with Mihail become heavily replayed footage once battlefield recordings are collected.

## Open questions

The fate of Host technology is unusual because essentially none remains physically available. Governments therefore possess battlefield recordings, sensor logs, human debris, damaged terrain, intelligence collected before dissolution, and memories from survivors — but almost no enemy material to seize and reverse-engineer.

The fourth-dimensional Kheruun world still exists unless otherwise established later. Mihail is the only confirmed human known to have gone there and returned after the final duel.

The exact long-term physical consequences of Mihail's exposure to the fourth-dimensional environment, the nuclear-like detonation he generated there, and the extreme overuse of his abilities remain unresolved at this stage.
