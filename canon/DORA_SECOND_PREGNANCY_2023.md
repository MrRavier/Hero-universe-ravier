# Dora's second pregnancy — 2023

Status: active canon.

After the Host War and Mihail I's recovery, Teodora "Dora" Orbelos becomes pregnant for a second time during 2023. Mihail and Dora already have one son, Aleksandar Mihail Orbelos, born in the second half of 2020 and first in the direct line of succession.

The pregnancy is first kept within the family and close household. Once it is publicly confirmed, the Royal Household makes only a short announcement and avoids turning the event into political propaganda or a symbol of the 2022 victory.

Aleksandar is told simply that he will have a younger sibling. His position does not change.

In the following months, routine prenatal care confirms that the second child is a girl. Mihail and Dora learn this privately before the information is shared with close family and later confirmed publicly.

The child's name, exact birth date, appearance and future Hability remain open.

Sintica has absolute primogeniture for future generations of the House of Orbelos. Once born, the daughter will follow her older brother Aleksandar in the succession.

The family preserves the same principle used with Aleksandar: the child is treated first as a child, not as a public experiment or symbolic object. Public speculation about inherited Habilities is not answered by the Palace.

For Mihail, the pregnancy has strong private meaning because it begins after the period in which he believed he might die during the Host War. He does not frame it publicly as destiny or miracle; within the family it is simply evidence that ordinary life continued after 2022.
