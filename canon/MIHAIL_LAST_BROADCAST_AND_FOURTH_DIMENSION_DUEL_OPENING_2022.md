# Mihail's Last Broadcast and the Opening of the Fourth-Dimension Duel (2022)

## Canon status
Active continuation of the Sahara / Last Superior war arc.

## Separation from the pseudo-Kheruun shell
When Mihail successfully teleports himself and the Last Superior out of the terrestrial battlefield and into the adjacent fourth-dimensional Kheruun world, the pseudo-Kheruun humanoids that had been clinging to the Superior do not make the transition as a coherent fighting shell.

For several seconds in the Sahara, the servant-built pseudo-armor remains suspended in the air around the point where the Superior had been, as though its organizing relation had been removed faster than gravity and local structure could react. The humanoid bodies then lose coordination and collapse in a heap onto the battlefield.

The implication is that the Superior's personal spatial relation was transferred, but the majority of the servant-shell was not.

## Initial spatial asymmetry
Mihail deliberately chooses a different arrival state for himself and for the Superior.

He places himself close to the fourth-dimensional ground while forcing the Superior's translated arrival relation to carry residual downward / forward motion high above that surface. The intention is not elegant teleportation but a crude tactical abuse of reference, momentum and relative placement.

The Superior therefore arrives in the fourth-dimensional world already falling.

He impacts the ruined Kheruun landscape violently.

Mihail remains standing on the ground at distance, injured and exhausted but conscious.

## The world around them
The place is consistent with Mihail's fragmentary stolen and remembered impressions: dark-red sky, gray and black plains, immense ruins, broken structures and the aftermath of the ancient Hipermatança Final do Crepúsculo do Mundo Material.

At this moment, there are only two active principal figures in the immediate scene:
- Mihail I Konstantin Orbelos;
- the Last Superior.

What happens elsewhere in this dimension is not established by this file.

## Mihail projects one final message to Earth
Before committing fully to the coming confrontation, Mihail forces one last psionic act despite extreme exhaustion.

Using the organized mental pathways he had previously established for long-distance contact, he projects his own visual perspective and voice into a live terrestrial television feed that is already carrying the Sahara battle.

The transmission is brief, unstable and visibly difficult for him to maintain.

Mihail does not explain what he learned inside his own mind, nor does he explain his plan.

He instead speaks as though he may not return.

He thanks humanity for having fought together despite division and rivalry, and says that whatever happens next, the fact that so many people chose to stand for one another mattered.

He apologizes specifically to Dora, to his mother Katalin, to his sister Maria Ilona, to Nikola, Sofia, Aleksandar and the rest of his family for leaving without certainty that he will come back.

He asks Dora to forgive him for choosing the dangerous course when he knew there was another life waiting for him at home.

He expresses love for his son Aleksandar and says that Sintica must remain his home rather than become a monument to his father.

He asks for peace, continuity and prosperity for the Principality of Sintica, regardless of what happens to him personally.

He also wishes the same for the wider world and says that, if humanity survives, it should remember that it had once stood together when extinction was placed before it as a choice.

He ends the transmission deliberately before emotion or exhaustion can break his focus.

The exact wording may vary in dramatization, but the canonical substance is: gratitude, apology, farewell, family, Sintica, humanity, peace and prosperity.

## No confirmed death
The message is framed by Mihail as a possible farewell, not proof that he intends to die and not confirmation that he will die.

His survival or death remains unresolved.

## The Superior rises
After the transmission is cut, Mihail turns his full attention back to the Last Superior.

The Superior begins to rise from the crater / impact zone, injured but alive.

Mihail does not wait for him to regain full composure.

He uses Éter to violently accelerate himself upward and forward, converting the ground beneath him into a launch point.

At the same time, Black Ink pours over one arm and hand, building not into a separate weapon but into an enormous reinforced fist around his own striking limb.

The result is a colossal black-red striking form around Mihail's hand and forearm.

From the Superior's perspective, the final clear image before the clash is Mihail coming down from above at extreme speed, body wrapped in turbulent Éter, one massive Black Ink fist leading the descent.

This file ends before impact.
