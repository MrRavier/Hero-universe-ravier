# Fourth Dimension — Greatest Atomic Explosion Observed by Humanity (2022)

## Canon point
Mihail Orbelos, after discovering that the ability previously classified as “Solid Oxygen” is actually a broader matter-manipulation ability that can reach molecular and, at extreme strain, atomic scale, surrounds the last Superior with an invisible line/volume in which he forces atomic-scale splitting and injects additional energy through his ability.

This is not a real-world nuclear weapon mechanism and should not be treated as one. In-universe, the effect is a supernatural, direct manipulation of matter and nuclear-scale binding rather than an engineered chain-reaction device.

## Scale
For Earth-equivalent comparison, the event is treated as roughly **100 megatons TNT equivalent**, making it larger than the ~50 Mt Tsar Bomba and therefore the largest nuclear-like explosion ever directly observed by humans.

Because the event occurs in the Kheruun fourth-dimensional world, exact blast, thermal, radiation and atmospheric behavior may differ from terrestrial nuclear physics. Earth-equivalent values are only a comparison scale.

At roughly 100 Mt Earth-equivalent, an Earth-like atmosphere would imply a fireball several kilometres across, catastrophic blast over tens of kilometres, and a visible flash and shock effects at enormous range.

## Mihail’s escape
Mihail does not remain only a few kilometres away: that would be unsurvivable even with a dense lead-like shell. He teleports **well over one hundred kilometres away** across the repetitive red wasteland, using the visually similar desolate terrain as an unusually easy reference field for long-range teleportation.

He coats himself in a dense lead-like layer as improvised protection against penetrating radiation, while relying on distance, terrain, Éter, armor and teleportation for the more dangerous heat and blast components.

## Event
From Mihail’s distant position, the horizon first turns white. The blast source becomes a false sun. The surface around the Superior disappears into an expanding incandescent sphere, followed by an enormous pressure front and a rising column of destroyed mineral and atmospheric material. Ruins are flattened outward in concentric devastation.

The Superior’s immediate condition after the blast remains unresolved at this canon point unless later established.

## Continuity
- The last Superior had already been wounded by conventional strategic bombing in the Sahara and by the prolonged duel with Mihail.
- Mihail had already transmitted a farewell to Earth before the final atomic-scale attack.
- The Host on Earth continues fighting during the event; the explosion occurs in the adjacent fourth-dimensional Kheruun world.
- Whether the Superior survives, whether Mihail survives intact, and what the blast does to the fourth-dimensional world beyond the local devastation remain open until explicitly decided later.
