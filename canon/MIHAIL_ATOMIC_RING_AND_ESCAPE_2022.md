# Mihail's Atomic Ring and Escape — 2022

In the fourth-dimensional Kheruun world, after discovering that his supposed 'solid oxygen' ability is actually a much broader matter-manipulation ability, Mihail escalates the confrontation with the Last Superior.

Mihail projects his matter manipulation not as a conventional visible attack but as a fictional, superhuman effect tracing an invisible ring around the Superior. He then adds energy to the effect, intending to turn the encirclement into a catastrophic high-energy trap. The exact real-world physical mechanism is not defined; this is an extraordinary ability, not a reproducible technical process.

Recognizing the danger of remaining nearby, Mihail also uses his matter control on himself to form a dense lead-like protective shell around his body as a last-ditch radiation/impact barrier. The shell is imperfect and improvised, serving primarily as a dramatic protective layer rather than a guarantee of survival.

Immediately after completing the encirclement, Mihail teleports several kilometers away across the desolate red landscape. The fourth-dimensional environment is easier for him to use as a teleport reference than Earth in this moment because the terrain is broadly homogeneous, visually open, and dominated by similar red-gray ruin fields, allowing him to select a distant but recognizable destination with less urban/structural ambiguity.

This moment establishes that Mihail is no longer merely improvising at molecular chemistry scale: under extreme stress, he is attempting a dangerous atomic-scale application of his true matter ability. The consequences of the trap, the Superior's survival, and Mihail's condition remain unresolved until the next canon event.
